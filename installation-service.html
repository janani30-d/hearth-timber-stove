GOOD TO KNOW

Your Questions, Answered

Find helpful information about stove installation, routine maintenance, and caring for your home heating system.

Do I need a professional stove installation?

Professional installation is strongly recommended for wood and pellet stoves. The installation should follow the appliance manufacturer's instructions and applicable local building and fire-safety requirements. Use an appropriately qualified installer for your appliance and location.

How often should my stove be serviced?

Service frequency depends on your stove model, how often you use it, the fuel quality, and the manufacturer's recommendations. Follow the maintenance schedule in your appliance manual and arrange chimney or flue inspections as required.

Can you service wood and pellet stoves?

Service requirements differ between wood-burning and pellet appliances. Contact our team with your stove type, brand, and model so we can discuss the appropriate service options.

What should I prepare before installation?

Have your preferred stove model, property details, and any existing chimney or flue information ready if available. The installer can then assess the proposed location, required clearances, ventilation, and other site-specific requirements.

How can I improve my stove's efficiency?

Use the fuel specified by the manufacturer, keep the appliance maintained, and follow the recommended operating instructions. Correct installation, suitable fuel, and a clean, properly functioning flue can all contribute to effective operation.

How do I request a service appointment?

Visit our contact page and tell us whether you need installation, routine maintenance, or help with an existing stove. Include the appliance type and model, if known, so our team can discuss the next steps with you.
