PLAN YOUR INSTALLATION

Understanding Installation Costs

The cost of installing a wood or pellet stove depends on your home, the selected appliance, and the work required to create a safe and effective heating setup.

01

Installation & Labor

Professional installation involves positioning the appliance, connecting the required components, and checking that the system is installed correctly.

What can affect the cost?
  • Installation complexity and labor time
  • Stove type and connection requirements
  • Accessibility of the installation area
02

Chimney & Venting

A suitable chimney or venting system is essential. Existing infrastructure, flue routing, and any necessary upgrades can influence the final estimate.

What can affect the cost?
  • Existing chimney condition and compatibility
  • Flue pipes, liners, and venting components
  • New chimney work or required modifications
03

Hearth & Site Preparation

Your installation area may need suitable floor protection, safe clearances, or other preparation before the appliance can be installed.

What can affect the cost?
  • Hearth or floor-protection requirements
  • Clearance and surrounding-surface adjustments
  • Site preparation and finishing work
i

Every Home Is Different

Installation requirements vary by property and appliance. A site assessment can help identify the work involved, suitable components, and any additional preparation needed. Final requirements should be confirmed by a qualified installation professional.

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful answers about stove pricing, installation, maintenance, and the costs to consider when choosing the right heating solution for your home.

How much does a wood or pellet stove cost?

The cost depends on the stove model, heating capacity, fuel type, design, and manufacturer. Contact our team to explore suitable options and receive current pricing based on your heating requirements.

What factors affect the installation cost?

Installation costs can vary depending on the stove type, existing chimney or venting system, hearth preparation, required materials, and the complexity of the work. A site assessment can help identify the requirements for your home.

Will I need a new chimney or venting system?

Not every installation requires a completely new chimney, but every stove needs an appropriate, compliant venting arrangement. The requirements depend on the appliance, the existing setup, and applicable building and safety regulations.

What is included in stove maintenance?

Depending on the service, maintenance may include inspecting the appliance, checking key components, cleaning relevant areas, and identifying wear or potential issues. The exact work and cost depend on your stove model and service requirements.

How often should my stove be inspected or serviced?

Inspection and servicing frequency depends on the appliance, how often it is used, the manufacturer's recommendations, and local safety requirements. Regular maintenance helps identify problems early and supports safe, efficient operation.

Can I get a pricing estimate before making a decision?

Yes. Contact our team with details about your space, preferred stove type, and installation requirements. We can discuss suitable options and explain the factors that may affect your overall project cost.

Are accessories included in the stove price?

Included accessories vary by stove model and supplier. Items such as hearth protection, venting components, stove tools, and other accessories may be priced separately. Ask our team for a breakdown of what is included with your selected appliance.
