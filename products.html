YOUR HOME, YOUR HEATING

How to Choose the Right Stove

The right stove depends on more than appearance. Consider your space, fuel preferences, installation requirements, and ongoing costs before making a decision.

Comfort begins with the right heating solution for your home.

01

Assess Your Space

Consider the room size, insulation, ceiling height, and layout. Choose a stove with a suitable heat output for your space.

02

Choose Your Fuel

Compare firewood and wood pellets based on local availability, storage space, handling, and the type of heating experience you prefer.

03

Check Installation Requirements

Review flue or chimney requirements, clearances, ventilation, and electrical needs where applicable. Have the installation assessed by a qualified professional.

04

Plan Your Budget

Consider the purchase price, installation, fuel, routine maintenance, and any additional components required for your setup.
